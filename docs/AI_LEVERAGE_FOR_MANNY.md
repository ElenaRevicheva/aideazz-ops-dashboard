# AI Leverage for Manny — Revenue Probability ($50k–$1M)

**Principle:** No AI theater. No fake CTO agents. No infrastructure circus.

**Question:** How do we use AI in a way that increases revenue probability for $50k–$1M projects?

---

## Manny's Real Leverage Points

Big construction deals are won or lost on:

- **Speed of response**
- **Professionalism of proposal**
- **Perceived competence**
- **Trust and follow-up discipline**
- **Consistent visibility**

AI must **support** those. Not replace people. Support leverage.

---

## 5 High-Impact AI Layers

### 1. AI Proposal Acceleration System (High ROI)

**Problem:** Construction owners waste hours rewriting similar proposals.

**Build:** "Proposal Draft Engine"

**Input:** Project type, scope summary, budget range, timeline, location, trade requirements

**AI outputs:**
- Structured proposal draft
- Scope breakdown, assumptions, timeline section
- Warranty language, capability statement insert
- Minority-owned positioning, crew capacity description

**Impact:** Proposal time 3 hours → 30 minutes. Speed wins big jobs.

**Stack:** Claude + structured prompt + reusable template. No overengineering.

---

### 2. AI Intake Intelligence (Filtering Big Clients)

**Enhance form submission flow:**
- AI summarizes the project
- Scores likelihood (small job vs serious project)
- Flags if budget seems large
- Suggests follow-up script

**Impact:** Manny calls high-value leads first. Protects his time.

**Stack:** Simple LLM + rules. No fancy ML.

---

### 3. AI Case Study Generator (Authority Builder)

**Input:** Project photos, scope notes, before/after, materials used, challenges, outcome

**AI generates:**
- Professional case study page
- LinkedIn post
- Short commercial-style description
- SEO-optimized blog article
- One-paragraph bid insert

**Impact:** One project → 5 marketing assets. Real leverage.

---

### 4. AI Follow-Up Discipline System

**Problem:** Most contractors lose deals because they stop following up.

**AI supports:**
- 3 structured follow-up emails
- 2 follow-up SMS templates
- Call notes summary
- Recap emails after meetings

**Impact:** Big clients appreciate professionalism. AI increases consistency.

---

### 5. AI Reputation Loop

**After project completion, AI drafts:**
- Review request
- Google review response
- Testimonial summary
- Referral ask message

**Impact:** Retention is built here.

---

These will not increase his revenue. They will increase stress.
